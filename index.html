gws-cli

gws-cli is a personal command-line tool used by a single developer to work with their own Google account from their own computer.

What it does

  • Signs in with the developer's own Google account through Google OAuth.
  • Reads, creates, edits, and deletes the developer's files in Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, and Google Slides.
  • Can read, organize, and send the developer's Gmail messages, and manage their Google Calendar events and Google Tasks.
  • Can use Google Cloud and Pub/Sub resources that belong to the developer's account.

Every action runs only when the developer issues a command. The tool is not offered to other users.
