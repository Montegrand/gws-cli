gws-cli

gws-cli is a personal command-line tool used by a single developer to read their own Google Drive files and Google Sheets from their own computer.

What it does

  • Signs in with the developer's own Google account through Google OAuth.
  • Requests read-only access to Google Drive and Google Sheets.
  • Reads files and spreadsheet values so the developer can use them in local work.

It does not create, change, or delete any file, and it is not offered to other users.
